About & Safety

What Is Tinnitus?

Tinnitus is the perception of sound — often a ringing, buzzing, or hissing — without an outside source producing it. It's very common and is usually not itself dangerous, though it can be distracting or distressing. It's often linked to changes in the hearing system, but its exact cause varies from person to person.

What This App Does — and Doesn't

EaseTinnitus offers three self-guided, sound-based tools — pitch matching, sound therapy, and a residual inhibition test — built on techniques used broadly in tinnitus self-management. Many people find sound-based approaches like these helpful for making their tinnitus less noticeable day to day.

It does not diagnose, treat, or cure tinnitus or any hearing condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care. It is not a medical device. Individual results vary, and some people won't notice any effect.

See a doctor or audiologist if your tinnitus started suddenly, affects only one ear, came with hearing loss, dizziness, or pain, or is getting noticeably worse — these can need prompt evaluation.
Your Data

Everything you enter — your matched pitch and loudness, and your session history — is stored only in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data, or the button below, will erase it.
