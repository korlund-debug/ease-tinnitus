💡 Suggested Use

Many people use sound therapy for 30–90 minutes a day, or overnight while sleeping. Start at a comfortable volume — around or just below the loudness of your tinnitus, never uncomfortably loud. There's no single "right" amount; consistency tends to matter more than duration.
