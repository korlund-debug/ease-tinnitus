EaseTinnitus
Sound therapy and self-guided tools that help many people manage tinnitus.
Please note: EaseTinnitus is an independent wellness tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose or cure tinnitus — it offers sound-based techniques some people find helpful for managing how noticeable their tinnitus feels. Results vary from person to person. If your tinnitus is sudden, one-sided, or comes with hearing loss, dizziness, or pain, please see a doctor or audiologist.
