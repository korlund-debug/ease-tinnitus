Residual Inhibition

Residual inhibition is a temporary quieting — or brief stopping — of tinnitus that some people notice after listening to matching sound for a short period. Not everyone experiences it, and any effect is usually short-lived.

⏱️ Recommended Usage

Try this once or twice a day at most. Listening at a comfortable volume matters more than listening longer — turning it up won't make the effect stronger and can be uncomfortable.
